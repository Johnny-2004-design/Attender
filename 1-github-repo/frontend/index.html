Everything a lecturer needs. Nothing a student has to learn.

Two roles, one verification engine.

QR or one-time code

Show a rotating QR in a corner of your laptop, print one, or write a six-character code on the board. Both go through the same checks.

GPS radius check

Set the classroom location and a radius (20 m, 30 m, 50 m or your own). Low-accuracy readings are flagged for review.

Face match + liveness

A live face is compared with the student’s registered template, with a random blink or head-turn challenge to defeat photos.

Register once per class

Students join through your invite link. Approved students are reused across your courses, with enrolment managed separately.

Corrections, never silent

Change a record and ATTENDER logs who, when, what changed and why. History is never overwritten.

PDF and Excel reports

Daily, course, class, semester and per-student reports with attendance percentages.
